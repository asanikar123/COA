01The Rail — our layout principle

The site is built on a left rail, not a centered column. A fixed-width left column holds full-bleed imagery; every line of text on the page shares one vertical left edge. Headlines, body copy, eyebrows, quotes, and buttons all begin on that line, flush beneath the hero text.

Image rail

At a 1440px screen this shared edge lands at ~437px. It is enforced by offsetting content with margin-left: var(--rail). The footer is the one intentional exception — it re-centers full width.
