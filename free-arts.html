Where Literature, Music & Ideas Come Alive

At Alphabet City, City of Asylum presents 120+ free programs each year — globally-focused literary readings, concerts, films, and festivals. All programs are also live-streamed free.

What We Host

Pittsburgh's Living Room to the World

Live in Alphabet City locally...and streamed to the world.

Our programming bridges cultures through four creative pillars — each one free, open to the public, and grounded in the belief that art builds understanding.

Literary Readings

Acclaimed and emerging writers in all genres, with a special focus on authors in translation. 4 Nobel Laureates in Literature, 13 Pulitzer Prize Winners, 8 National Book Awardees, 11 MacArthur Fellows, and 4 U.S. Poet Laureates.

Music & Performance

National and international jazz groups on tour, world music, a bit of classical/new music, and Thursday Night Jazz by Pittsburgh's finest musicians debuting new work.

A Home for
Other Nonprofits

At no cost we provide Alphabet City performance space to arts organizations who share our mission and have no facility of their own. A potpourri of film, poetry, storytelling, and surprises.

Unique to City of Asylum

Multi-lingual Jazz-Poetry collaborations, Allegheny County Poet Laureate & Youth Poet Laureate programs, Festivals, ASL Poetry and more.

Why jazz in addition to literary readings?

Jazz is about giving voice to each individual in an evolving but coherent ensemble framework. In many ways it is a model of the community we envision. Jazz has also been the music associated with civil rights and freedom in the U.S.

Why Our Programs Are Free

Giving Voice and Opening Ears

Removing barriers to creative expression —

so everyone can listen, learn, and belong.

At Alphabet City, we remove barriers of access. Artists on our stage are paid, but tickets to every program are free. There are no reserved seats or dress codes, and there are many accommodations for those living with disabilities.

When people from all walks of life are in the audience, the experience bridges social and cultural differences and builds community, along with the joy of the art.

Our revenue comes from mission-based donations in campaigns — not ticket sales — support that is purposeful rather than transactional.
