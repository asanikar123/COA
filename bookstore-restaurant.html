Bookstore

City of Asylum Bookstore

City of Asylum Bookstore is the largest bookstore in the United States dedicated to literature in translation and the work of writers the world has tried to silence. Our shelves include international fiction and poetry, children's books, and cookbooks from around the world. Every purchase supports our free arts and culture programs.

Online you can order virtually any book in print as well as audible and e-books.

Restaurant

Cucina Alfabeto

Cucina Alfabeto is the restaurant inside Alphabet City. It is open for dinner Wednesday to Sunday and brunch on weekends.

Check City of Asylum’s calendar of concerts and readings. As always, programs are free. During a program, you can watch from your table while you dine. You are also welcome to take a drink to your seat in the performance space.
