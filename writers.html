The Exiled Writer Residency Program

These are voices the world tried to silence.

But at City of Asylum, those voices
are heard.

Every writer-in-residence has written and published despite exile — most a full-length work.

Our residency program provides:

Safe Home In one of our 7 houses on Sampsonia Way, Pittsburgh
Living Stipend Financial support for three years or more
Legal Counsel Immigration and legal support for writers and families, when necessary
Medical Benefits Healthcare for writers and families during their residency
Career Support Translation and publishing, next step planning
Social Services A full-time residency manager coordinates individual needs and community integration
Long-term Safety More than emergency support.

“I admire what [City of Asylum] has done to accommodate the writers to an extraordinary degree after their residency
is up.”
— Russell Banks
Author, President International Parliament of Writers

Fellowship for Ukrainian Writers

In response to the 2022 invasion of Ukraine, City of Asylum launched a fellowship enabling three Ukrainian writers — Olena Boryshpolets, Oleksandr Frazé-Frazénko, and Volodymyr Rafeyenko — to come to Pittsburgh so they can continue to research, write, and publish despite the ongoing war.

Haiti Current

Bertony Louis

Poet · 2025

A poet, educator, and lawyer whose humanitarian work bridges cultures, languages, and continents. Winner of 13 international poetry awards. In exile due to the threats of a failed state.

Ethiopia Former

Bewketu Seyoum

Poet, Novelist, Essayist · 2019–2021

A novelist, poet, comedian who satirized political corruption and social injustice, he is one of the leading writers of his generation. He was honored as Ethiopian Best Novelist of the Year and Best Young Author.

Sudan Current

Rania Mamoun

Novelist, Poet, Activist · Since 2019

Her journalism, fiction, poetry, and activism confronted issues of censorship, social justice, and women’s rights. She was a leader in the Women’s Parliament and the Sudanese resistance committee.

Ukraine Former

Volodymyr Rafeyenko

Ukrainian Fellowship · 2023–2026

An award-winning novelist, poet, translator, and literary critic, he wrote and published entirely in Russian until the Russian invasion of Donetsk. He now writes only in Ukrainian.

Ukraine Current

Olena Boryshpolets

Ukrainian Fellowship · 2023

Poet, writer, journalist, actress, and cultural organizer. Known for her vivid voice and interdisciplinary artistic work, which evokes the spirit and cultural memory of Odesa.

Ukraine Current

Oleksandr Frazé-Frazénko

Ukrainian Fellowship · 2023

A filmmaker, poet, musician, and translator, he is co-founder of OFF Laboratory, which publishes and translates voices silenced by war, exile, and censorship.

Cuba Former

Jorge Olivera Castillo

Writer · 2021–2024

Poet, journalist, and dissident, his writing led to his arrest during Cuba’s “Black Spring” crackdown. He spent 21 months in Guantanamo Prison, including 9 months in solitary, and re-arrested repeatedly.

Bangladesh Former

Tuhin Das

Poet · 2016–2021

A poet and essayist whose secular writings made him a target of Al Qaeda-linked militants murdering writers in Bangladesh. Forced into hiding, he lived underground and wrote clandestinely.

Syria Former

Osama Alomar

Writer · 2017–2019

One of the Arab world’s most celebrated practitioners of the “very short story” — parable-like fictions that skewer authoritarianism with dark humor. Forced to flee by the Assad regime.

Burma Former

Khet Mar

Writer · 2009–2012

A revered Burmese journalist and writer with 31,000 Facebook followers, she was sentenced to ten years imprisonment and freed from death-row during an amnesty. People made pilgrimages to visit her on Sampsonia Way from all over the U.S.

China Former

Huang Xiang

Poet · First Writer, 2004–2006

Known as the Walt Whitman of China, he was imprisoned for 12 years and tortured, and all his publications were destroyed or repressed by the government. He created City of Asylum's iconic “House Poem” and helped us re-imagine our mission.

El Salvador Former

Horacio Castellanos Moya

Writer · 2006–2011

His novel Revulsion, a furious satire of his native El Salvador, resulted in death threats. During his residency at City of Asylum he won one of the most significant literary awards—for lifetime achievement—in the Spanish-speaking world.

Venezuela Former

Israel Centeno

Writer · 2011–2018

One of Venezuela's most celebrated novelists and winner of the Federico García Lorca Award in Spain, he was forced into exile after his novel The Conspiracy drew threats and attacks from the Chávez regime.

Republic of Congo Former

Aunel Arneth

Writer · 2021–2022

A pro-democracy activist, journalist, and documentary filmmaker, his work exposed state crimes and government corruption. His documentary Keep Quiet or Die led to arrest warrants.

Vietnam Former

Mai Khoi

Writer · 2020–2023

A singer and composer—winner of international awards for art and activism—known as the “Lady Gaga of Vietnam,” she ran for the National Assembly on a pro-democracy platform. Police raids and evictions forced her into exile.

Cuba Former

Orlando Pardo Lazo

Writer · 2013

Novelist, photographer, and dissident blogger, his novel Boring Home was censored by the Cuban government in 2009, after which he was arrested three times and under constant watch by Castro's secret police.

Iraq Former

Sabreen Kadhim

Writer · 2016

Poet and television journalist whose work bore witness to violence during the rise of militias in Baghdad. Winner of the 3rd UNESCO Poetry Contest and the Iraqi Youth Poetry Festival.

Turkey Former

Simten Cosar

Writer · 2020–2022

A feminist political scientist and scholarly editor, she was persecuted for signing the “We Will Not Be a Party to this Crime” declaration that opposed military violence against Kurds.

Iran Former

Yaghoub Yadali

Writer · 2013–2015

An experienced TV director, his debut novel Rituals of Restlessness won Iran's most prestigious literary award. But subsequently—even though it had passed state censorship—it led to his imprisonment for depicting an adulterous affair.

Algeria Former

Anouar Rahmani

Writer · 2022–2026

Novelist, journalist, and the first public advocate for legalization of same-sex marriage in Algeria, he was shortlisted for the Index on Censorship’s Freedom of Expression Awards.

Egypt Current

Mukhtar Shehata

Writer · 2024

A novelist, ethnographer, and documentary filmmaker whose work explores social change, class, gender, and rural transformation in Egypt after the 2011 revolution and Arab identity.

Mexico Current

Anonymous

Writer · 2026

An acclaimed investigative journalist and author who has spent two decades covering narco- and human trafficking on the Mexico-U.S. border and its impact on marginalized communities.
